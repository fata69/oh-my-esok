# Oh My Esok design

Preserve the light/dark surfaces, hierarchy, grouped lists, and glass navigation of the installed preview 2. The exploratory Material 3 redesign, colored summary cards, grouped columns, and connecting line chart were rejected. Keep the main balance prominent, and present income and expense as plain figures on the existing surface, with no individual boxes.

Keep **Oh My Esok** as the root header. The floating glass dock has four destinations: Hari, Uang, Laporan, Setelan. Preserve the build 17 proportions: 56 dp at normal text size, with its bottom 6 dp above the Android safe area. On Hari and Uang, position the separate round 52 dp add button 8 dp to its right, with their vertical centers aligned. Use a single damped spring for the capsule opening and the circle emerging from behind it. On reversal, continue from the current position and speed. On Laporan and Setelan, let the capsule take up that space again and disable the outgoing action immediately. Respect the system's reduced motion setting. Preserve the selected day when adding a daily note.

Dark mode uses a pure black canvas (`#000000`) with neutral charcoal controls and dock material. Add color to the four navigation glyphs, and use weight and a sliding neutral capsule for the selected state. The glyphs have darker variants on light surfaces and brighter variants on AMOLED. Keep blue and orange for financial meaning in the ledger, paired with arrow direction and text. Leave glass surfaces neutral in both themes.

On a tab tap, move one neutral selection capsule with a thin, visible edge and a critically damped spring. A second tap retargets it from its current position and velocity. The capsule settles in roughly 170–200 ms, and reduced motion skips the glide. Icons and labels stay anchored to their tab cells. `+` keeps its separate reveal motion; this does not change the page transition.

Keep financial information on one surface: a prominent balance, separate wallet and transaction sections, and simple icons to distinguish ledger entry types. Use familiar list rows and a checkmark for model selection; show the display name and canonical Google ID and make the selected, empty, and failed states clear. Keep the saved choice on cancel or failure. Avoid decorative glass in content.

Use type size, weight and spacing to separate the context, main value, and supporting text. In Hari, Uang, and Laporan, align period titles left and group previous/next controls on the right. Use one neutral surface per agenda, wallet group, transaction day, or settings section, with inset dividers between rows. Do not box individual amounts, stack decorative surfaces, or alter the dock geometry to create hierarchy.

In Uang, place marked **Dompet** and **Transaksi** headings below the total. The transaction filter changes only the history in the selected month; the summary and wallet balances keep their full values. Use [Copilot Money's transaction filters](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/9554412-transactions-tab-overview) as a reference for this separation.

Use **Dompet** everywhere for where money sits or moves and **Kategori** for what a transaction was for. In Settings, explain the two side by side under **Keuangan**. Offer a short catalog of recognizable glyphs by transaction type rather than a separate glyph for each food or drink. Keep an automatic default and give the icon a neutral background. Existing saved selections remain usable.

Keep **Pindai struk** labeled and near **Simpan** at the bottom of the transaction editor. Reserve room for the entire keypad above the action row; the form scrolls independently on small screens and the keypad uses two rows on wide screens. Hide the custom keypad while the native text keyboard has focus and let the form scroll on exceptionally short viewports. The category field and its Batal/Tambah actions sit in one compact row when space allows; a larger text setting can wrap the actions below. Scan results stay editable drafts until Simpan.

Use the offline [build 18 design review](../design-review/oh-my-esok-design.html) as a baseline and compare the local candidate before release. The motion follows [Apple's guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion) on short, purposeful motion and reduced motion; the arrangement and styling belong to Oh My Esok. Review usual portrait sizes (360, 390, 412 dp at normal text) first; log extreme widths and text scaling as supplemental stress checks.

For report plots, use `fl_chart: ^1.3.1` from the lockfile. The compact volume view plots income as received and expense with its effect on cash flow (negative of the recorded expense). Signed imported values retain their meaning; when both marks share a side, draw them side by side to keep both measurable. Display exact recorded amounts separately. Include zeros in the date series; never count transfers or corrections as income or expense. Remove plots and numeric accessibility values when the user's privacy setting is enabled.

Use blue/orange accents with theme-specific contrast: light `#1558D4` / `#B64A0B`, dark `#548CFF` / `#FF8836`. Use signs/labels, paired with numbers and volume direction. The Add control matches the dock's material and has a short accessible action label.

References: the owner's Tether Wallet screenshots, [Apple's guidance for glass materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials), [toolbars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars?changes=la) and [buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons), [TradingView chart examples](https://tradingview.github.io/lightweight-charts/examples), and [fl_chart](https://pub.dev/packages/fl_chart/versions/1.3.1). These guide arrangement and interaction; the implementation uses Oh My Esok's own UI and data.
